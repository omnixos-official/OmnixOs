The project

Technology should
feel open to everyone.

Omnix OS is a planned free operating system project built around a simple idea: one welcoming desktop can support every kind of person.

Our mission

Build an Ubuntu-based KDE Plasma desktop that is beautiful, understandable, dependable, and easy to make your own.

All-in-one, not overloaded

Omnix starts clean. People choose optional Everyday, Study, Developer, Creator, Gaming, and Privacy packs as needed.

Designed for the future

Orbit UI brings a distinct, calm visual identity, while Omnix Hub and optional AI concepts aim to make the system simpler to use.
